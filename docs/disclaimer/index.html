6. The law is unsettled and still commencing

The DPDP framework is being brought into force in stages, and important pieces are still missing:

  • Substantive obligations on businesses do not commence until 14 May 2027.
  • No Significant Data Fiduciary has been notified under section 10(1).
  • No country has been restricted under section 16(1).
  • No startup relief has been notified under section 17(3).
  • The word "significant" in section 33(1), which gates every penalty, is undefined.
  • There is no Data Protection Board jurisprudence at all.

Nothing here can predict how the Board or the courts will interpret the Act. Any statement about likely enforcement outcomes is speculation, and penalty figures quoted are statutory ceilings, not predictions.

10. What you should actually do

If you need to know your obligations under the DPDP Act:

  1. Read the statutory text, and verify it against the Gazette of India.
  2. Check the current commencement notifications, because staggered commencement means an obligation may not yet be live.
  3. Check whether a sectoral regulator imposes stricter requirements on you.
  4. Engage qualified Indian legal counsel on your specific facts.

Use this repository to prepare for that conversation, not to replace it.
