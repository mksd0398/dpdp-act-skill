Summary (maintainers', not statutory text): No civil court jurisdiction over Board matters; no injunctions

## Section 39. Bar of jurisdiction

No civil court shall have the jurisdiction to entertain any suit or proceeding in respect of any matter for which the
Board is empowered under the provisions of this Act and no injunction shall be granted by any court or other authority
in respect of any action taken or to be taken in pursuance of any power under the provisions of this Act.
