Summary (maintainers', not statutory text): Centre may require info from the Board, any Data Fiduciary or intermediary

## Section 36. Power to call for information

The Central Government may, for the purposes of this Act, require the Board and any Data Fiduciary or intermediary to
furnish such information as it may call for.
