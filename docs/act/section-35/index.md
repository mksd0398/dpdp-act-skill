## Section 35. Protection of action taken in good faith

No suit, prosecution or other legal proceedings shall lie against the Central Government, the Board, its Chairperson
and any Member, officer or employee thereof for anything which is done or intended to be done in good faith under the
provisions of this Act or the rules made thereunder.
