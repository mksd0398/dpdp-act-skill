# DPDP Act 2023 full text: all 44 sections, section by section

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023), assented 11 August 2023. 44 sections in 9 chapters plus a Schedule of penalties.

- [Section 1. Short title and commencement](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-1/index.md): Staggered commencement permitted; different dates for different provisions
- [Section 2. Definitions](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-2/index.md): (a) Appellate Tribunal = TDSAT, (f) child = under 18, (i) Data Fiduciary, (j) Data Principal, (k) Data Processor, (t) personal data, (u) personal data breach, (x) processing, (y) "she" is gender-neutral, (z) SDF
- [Section 3. Application of Act](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-3/index.md): India + extraterritorial for goods/services offering; excludes personal/domestic use and self-published data
- [Section 4. Grounds for processing personal data](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-4/index.md): Only consent or s.7 legitimate uses; lawful purpose = not expressly forbidden by law
- [Section 5. Notice](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-5/index.md): Itemised notice with/before consent request; retrospective notice for pre-Act consent; English or Eighth Schedule language
- [Section 6. Consent](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-6/index.md): Free/specific/informed/unconditional/unambiguous + clear affirmative action; severability; easy withdrawal; Consent Managers; burden of proof on Fiduciary (6(10))
- [Section 7. Certain legitimate uses](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-7/index.md): Closed list (a)-(i): voluntary provision, State subsidy, State function, legal disclosure, court orders, medical emergency, epidemic, disaster, employment
- [Section 8. General obligations of Data Fiduciary](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-8/index.md): (1) accountability, (2) processor contract, (3) accuracy, (4) TOMs, (5) security, (6) breach notice to Board + each individual, (7) erasure, (8)+(11) deemed purpose expiry, (9) publish contact, (10) grievance mechanism
- [Section 9. Processing of personal data of children](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-9/index.md): Verifiable parental consent; no detrimental processing; no tracking/behavioural monitoring/targeted ads; Rules may exempt (1) and (3) only; s.9(5) age notification
- [Section 10. Additional obligations of Significant Data Fiduciary](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-10/index.md): Government-notified only; DPO in India responsible to the board; independent data auditor; periodic DPIA and audit
- [Section 11. Right to access information about personal data](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-11/index.md): Summary + processing activities + who it was shared with; law-enforcement sharing carved out
- [Section 12. Right to correction and erasure of personal data](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-12/index.md): Correct, complete, update on request; erase unless needed for purpose or law
- [Section 13. Right of grievance redressal](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-13/index.md): Fiduciary/Consent Manager must respond in prescribed period; must exhaust before going to the Board
- [Section 14. Right to nominate](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-14/index.md): Nominee exercises rights on death or incapacity
- [Section 15. Duties of Data Principal](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-15/index.md): No impersonation, no suppression of material info, no false or frivolous complaints, only authentic info; penalty up to Rs 10,000
- [Section 16. Processing of personal data outside India](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-16/index.md): Negative list: Centre may restrict named countries; higher standards in other Indian law preserved
- [Section 17. Exemptions](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-17/index.md): (1) six partial exemptions, s.8(1) and s.8(5) always survive; (2) total exemptions for notified State bodies and research/archiving/statistics; (3) startup relief from s.5, s.8(3), s.8(7), s.10, s.11; (4) State retention/correction carve-out; (5) five-year sunset power
- [Section 18. Establishment of Board](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-18/index.md): Body corporate; HQ notified by Centre
- [Section 19. Composition and qualifications for appointment of Chairperson and Members](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-19/index.md): Chairperson + notified Members, Centre-appointed; at least one legal expert
- [Section 20. Salary, allowances payable to and term of office](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-20/index.md): Two-year term, re-appointable; terms not variable to disadvantage
- [Section 21. Disqualifications for appointment and continuation as Chairperson and Members of Board](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-21/index.md): Insolvency, moral turpitude, incapacity, conflicting interest, abuse of position; hearing before removal
- [Section 22. Resignation by Members and filling of vacancy](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-22/index.md): Three-month notice effect; one-year post-office employment cooling-off with disclosure
- [Section 23. Proceedings of Board](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-23/index.md): Digital meetings permitted; acts not invalid for vacancy or procedural irregularity
- [Section 24. Officers and employees of Board](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-24/index.md): Appointed with Centre's approval
- [Section 25. Members and officers to be public servants](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-25/index.md): Deemed public servants under IPC s.21
- [Section 26. Powers of Chairperson](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-26/index.md): Superintendence, authorising scrutiny, allocating proceedings to Members
- [Section 27. Powers and functions of Board](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-27/index.md): Acts on breach intimation, Data Principal complaint, Centre/State reference, court direction, Consent Manager breach, s.37(2) intermediary reference; can issue binding directions after hearing
- [Section 28. Procedure to be followed by Board](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-28/index.md): Digital by design; sufficient-grounds filter; natural justice; CPC powers of summons/evidence/discovery/inspection; cannot bar premises or seize equipment; interim orders; costs for frivolous complaints
- [Section 29. Appeal to Appellate Tribunal](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-29/index.md): TDSAT; 60 days, condonable; endeavour six months; onward appeal via TRAI Act s.18
- [Section 30. Orders passed by Appellate Tribunal to be executable as decree](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-30/index.md): Enforced as a civil-court decree, or transmitted to a civil court
- [Section 31. Alternate dispute resolution](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-31/index.md): Board may direct mediation
- [Section 32. Voluntary undertaking](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-32/index.md): Accepted at any stage of s.28 proceedings; bars proceedings on its contents; breach reopens s.33
- [Section 33. Penalties](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-33/index.md): Only if the breach is "significant"; Schedule amounts are ceilings; seven mitigating/aggravating factors in s.33(2)
- [Section 34. Crediting sums realised by way of penalties to Consolidated Fund of India](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-34/index.md): All penalties go to the Consolidated Fund of India, not to victims
- [Section 35. Protection of action taken in good faith](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-35/index.md): No suit or prosecution against Centre, Board, Members, officers for good-faith acts
- [Section 36. Power to call for information](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-36/index.md): Centre may require info from the Board, any Data Fiduciary or intermediary
- [Section 37. Power of Central Government to issue directions](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-37/index.md): After two or more penalties, Centre may order blocking of public access; intermediaries bound
- [Section 38. Consistency with other laws](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-38/index.md): Additional to other laws; DPDP prevails on conflict
- [Section 39. Bar of jurisdiction](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-39/index.md): No civil court jurisdiction over Board matters; no injunctions
- [Section 40. Power to make rules](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-40/index.md): Roughly 26 delegated matters, (a) to (z)
- [Section 41. Laying of rules and certain notifications](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-41/index.md): 30-day laying before both Houses for s.16 and s.42 notifications
- [Section 42. Power to amend Schedule](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-42/index.md): Centre may amend, but cannot more than double any original penalty
- [Section 43. Power to remove difficulties](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-43/index.md): Orders within three years of commencement, laid before Parliament
- [Section 44. Amendments to certain Acts](https://mksd0398.github.io/dpdp-act-skill/act/section-44/index.md): TRAI Act s.14(c); IT Act s.43A and s.87(2)(ob) omitted, s.81 proviso amended; RTI Act s.8(1)(j) replaced
