**CHAPTER VII: APPEAL AND ALTERNATE DISPUTE RESOLUTION.** The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act 22 of 2023), section 30. Verbatim from the Gazette of India.

Summary (maintainers', not statutory text): Enforced as a civil-court decree, or transmitted to a civil court

## Section 30. Orders passed by Appellate Tribunal to be executable as decree

(1) An order passed by the Appellate Tribunal under this Act shall be executable by it as a decree of civil court, and
for this purpose, the Appellate Tribunal shall have all the powers of a civil court.

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-section (1), the Appellate Tribunal may transmit any order made by it to
a civil court having local jurisdiction and such civil court shall execute the order as if it were a decree made by
that court.
