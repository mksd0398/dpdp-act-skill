# DPDP 2023 section index (fast lookup)

One line per provision. Use this to find the right section, then read the verbatim text in
`act-full-text.md` before quoting. Never cite a section from this index alone.

## Chapter I: Preliminary (s.1-3)
| s. | Marginal note | One line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Short title and commencement | Staggered commencement permitted; different dates for different provisions |
| 2 | Definitions | (a) Appellate Tribunal = TDSAT, (f) child = under 18, (i) Data Fiduciary, (j) Data Principal, (k) Data Processor, (t) personal data, (u) personal data breach, (x) processing, (y) "she" is gender-neutral, (z) SDF |
| 3 | Application of Act | India + extraterritorial for goods/services offering; excludes personal/domestic use and self-published data |

## Chapter II: Obligations of Data Fiduciary (s.4-10)
| s. | Marginal note | One line |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Grounds for processing | Only consent or s.7 legitimate uses; lawful purpose = not expressly forbidden by law |
| 5 | Notice | Itemised notice with/before consent request; retrospective notice for pre-Act consent; English or Eighth Schedule language |
| 6 | Consent | Free/specific/informed/unconditional/unambiguous + clear affirmative action; severability; easy withdrawal; Consent Managers; **burden of proof on Fiduciary (6(10))** |
| 7 | Certain legitimate uses | Closed list (a)-(i): voluntary provision, State subsidy, State function, legal disclosure, court orders, medical emergency, epidemic, disaster, employment |
| 8 | General obligations | (1) accountability, (2) processor contract, (3) accuracy, (4) TOMs, (5) security, (6) breach notice to Board + each individual, (7) erasure, (8)+(11) deemed purpose expiry, (9) publish contact, (10) grievance mechanism |
| 9 | Children | Verifiable parental consent; no detrimental processing; **no tracking/behavioural monitoring/targeted ads**; Rules may exempt (1) and (3) only; s.9(5) age notification |
| 10 | Significant Data Fiduciary | Government-notified only; DPO in India responsible to the board; independent data auditor; periodic DPIA and audit |

## Chapter III: Rights and duties of Data Principal (s.11-15)
| s. | Marginal note | One line |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | Right to access | Summary + processing activities + who it was shared with; law-enforcement sharing carved out |
| 12 | Right to correction and erasure | Correct, complete, update on request; erase unless needed for purpose or law |
| 13 | Right of grievance redressal | Fiduciary/Consent Manager must respond in prescribed period; **must exhaust before going to the Board** |
| 14 | Right to nominate | Nominee exercises rights on death or incapacity |
| 15 | Duties of Data Principal | No impersonation, no suppression of material info, no false or frivolous complaints, only authentic info; penalty up to Rs 10,000 |
